About

Ideafolio LLC is an independent software studio based in Weehawken, New Jersey.

We design and build small, focused apps for iPhone. Each one sets out to do a single job well, with as little setup and as few settings as possible, and without accounts, ads or tracking.

Our first app, Shoebox, moves screenshots out of the camera roll the moment they're taken and keeps them in one searchable place on your iPhone. TypeReader is coming next.
