Shoebox · Effective November 10, 2026

Privacy policy

Shoebox keeps your screenshots on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. We don't collect, see or share any of your data.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that does any of these.
  • No servers: Shoebox doesn't send your screenshots or anything about you to Ideafolio LLC or anyone else.
  • iCloud Sync uses your own iCloud account. Ideafolio LLC can't see what's stored there.

What Shoebox uses, and why

Your photo library

With your permission, Shoebox looks for screenshots in your photo library so it can copy them into the app. It only reads items that iOS marks as screenshots. It removes screenshots from your photo library only when you ask it to, and iOS asks you to confirm. Removed items stay in Photos' Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Shoebox never moves or deletes your other photos.

If you choose “Save to Photos” on a screenshot, Shoebox adds that one image back to your library.

The “Save to Shoebox” shortcut

The shortcut runs on your iPhone in Apple's Shortcuts app. It hands each new screenshot to Shoebox and, depending on your setting, removes it from your photo library afterwards.

Images you share to Shoebox

If you share an image to Shoebox from another app, Shoebox saves a copy in the app.

Text in your screenshots

To make screenshots searchable, Shoebox recognizes text in them using Apple's on-device text recognition. This happens entirely on your iPhone.

Where your data is stored

Screenshots, the text recognized in them and your settings are stored in Shoebox's storage on your iPhone. Like any app's data, it's included in your iPhone backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple and your device, not by us.

iCloud Sync

With iCloud Sync on (Shoebox Settings → iCloud Sync, on by default), Shoebox keeps a copy of your library in your private iCloud, so it's safe if you lose your iPhone and appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It uses Apple's CloudKit and counts toward your iCloud storage. Ideafolio LLC has no access to it.

The text recognized in your screenshots, your notes and tags, and links to where a screenshot came from are end-to-end encrypted: only your devices can read them, not Apple and not us. The images and basic details such as dates and sizes are stored encrypted by Apple in iCloud.

Your settings and which screenshots came from which device's photo library stay on each device and never sync.

Deleting your data

Delete screenshots inside Shoebox at any time. With iCloud Sync on, a screenshot you delete is also deleted from iCloud and your other devices.

Turning off iCloud Sync stops syncing and keeps your library on your iPhone. To delete Shoebox's copy in iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (or See All) → Shoebox, and delete its data.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. A copy in iCloud stays until you delete it there.

Children

Shoebox doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If Shoebox ever starts handling data differently, we'll say so here before it does.
