Set up automatic capture

Shoebox uses a shortcut, Save to Shoebox, to move each new screenshot out of Photos the moment you take it. You add it once.

  1. During setup, tap the Save to Shoebox tile. Later, you'll find it in Shoebox → Settings → Set Up Shortcut.
  2. In the sheet that opens in Shortcuts, turn on Automation, then tap Add Shortcut.
  3. If Add Shortcut doesn't respond, tap Skip Setup instead. It still adds the shortcut. This is a known issue in iOS 27.
  4. Take a screenshot. Once it's saved to Photos, it shows up in Shoebox within a few seconds and leaves Photos.

Saving a screenshot to Photos

Shoebox picks a screenshot up once it's saved to Photos. How it gets saved depends on your iPhone's screenshot setting.

To have screenshots save on their own, turn off Full-Screen Previews in iPhone Settings → General → Screen Capture.

Nothing shows up in Shoebox

  • If the screenshot opened full screen, tap the checkmark and choose Save to Photos. Shoebox only sees screenshots that are saved to Photos. See Saving a screenshot to Photos.
  • If Shoebox's Settings shows Automation Looks Paused, the last few screenshots reached Photos without the automation running. Tap Open Automations there and check the next point.
  • Open Shortcuts → Automation and check that When a screenshot is saved is turned on. iOS adds shared automations switched off, so this is the most common cause.
  • Check that Save to Shoebox is in Shortcuts. If it's missing, add it again from Shoebox → Settings → Set Up Shortcut.
  • Check that Shoebox can see your photos: iPhone Settings → Apps → Shoebox → Photos → Full Access.
  • If you deleted the Shortcuts app, get it again from the App Store. Shoebox needs it to capture screenshots automatically.

Even without the shortcut, Shoebox picks up new screenshots from Photos each time you open it, and asks before removing them from Photos.

Common questions

Why did my screenshot leave Photos?

That's Shoebox doing its job: new screenshots move out of Photos and into Shoebox. Removed ones stay in Photos' Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Can I keep screenshots in Photos to share them from other apps?

Yes. In Shoebox → Settings → Remove from Photos, choose After 1 Hour, After 1 Day, After 1 Week, or Never. Shoebox still saves each screenshot right away.

Some screenshots are in both Photos and Shoebox.

Screenshots taken while Remove from Photos was set to Never stay in Photos on purpose. To clear them, open Settings → Screenshots in Photos → Duplicates and tap Remove from Photos. Shoebox keeps its copies.

How do I get a screenshot back into Photos?

Open it in Shoebox and choose Save to Photos.

Do my screenshots sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud. iCloud Sync is on by default and keeps your library the same on every iPhone signed in to your Apple Account. Turn it off in Shoebox → Settings → iCloud Sync; your screenshots stay on that iPhone either way. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

I set up Shoebox on a second iPhone. Why aren't its screenshots moving?

Your library syncs, but the automation doesn't: Shortcuts keeps automations on each device. On the second iPhone, open Shoebox → Settings → Set Up Shortcut and turn on Automation there too.

What happens if I delete Shoebox?

With iCloud Sync on, your screenshots stay in your iCloud and come back when you install Shoebox again and choose Use iCloud library. With iCloud Sync off, deleting the app deletes the screenshots stored in it, so save any you want to keep back to Photos first.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later, and Apple's Shortcuts app for automatic capture.
