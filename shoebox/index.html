How it works

Shoebox comes with a shortcut for the Shortcuts app. Add it once, switch it on, and every new screenshot moves into Shoebox on its own. Want to keep screenshots in Photos for a while so you can share them from other apps? Choose how long in Settings.

Screenshots already in your camera roll? Shoebox shows how many there are and how much space they take, and moves them over when you say so. Removing them from Photos always asks first.
